Chinese Investment Tracker – Turkey

Project dossiers of investment by mainland Chinese, Hong Kong and Chinese-owned foreign companies in Turkey, reconstructed from the Dünya newspaper archive (). Every project is backed by quoted sentences from the articles. Investment evidence is kept separate from company news, contractor work and general statements.
A project is shown when it has at least one dated investment-evidence or mention item inside the window.
