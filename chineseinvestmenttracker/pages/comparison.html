Chinese investment in Turkey, Hungary and Kazakhstan, compared

The trackers register Chinese investment projects from each country's own press with one set of rules: a project is a Chinese, Hong Kong or Macau investor tied to a site or a target, and each project is followed from its first report to a plant, a dormant promise or an exit. The charts set the registers side by side.

At a glance

What became of the projects

Realised: operating or under way as last reported. Live promise: announced, agreed, in talks or rumoured, with a report inside the dormancy window. Dormant promise: no report found for the window or longer. Failed: bid lost or not made, abandoned or on hold, or the investor exited. Counts are projects, not money.
